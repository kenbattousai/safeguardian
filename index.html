About this prototype

SafeGuardian · In-app Messaging v2.3 · 1 Oct 2026

Working prototype (Hazard module). The reviewer journey now starts from a device notification, and the Pending list ages unreviewed hazards yellow (day 4), orange (day 7) and red (day 14). Online means the person is logged in (Singpass 30 minutes, biometrics 24 hours) and has the app open in the foreground. Status is checked every 5 minutes. Both phones share one live backend.

      How are you testing?

      Two phones: pick Reviewer here and Submitter on the other phone (or the other way round). Both join one live session, so a message sent on one phone arrives on the other.

      One phone: pick “Both roles on this phone” and switch between them with the ⇄ button.

      Both phones must use the same session name. Use a new name to run a separate test.
